Data scope
----------
The integrated predictor currently uses verified OJEE 2024 LE-to-B.Tech records filtered to HS / General / Gender Neutral seats. TFW/EWS/category-specific records are not mixed into the General Rank predictor.

The predictor has two modes:
1. I KNOW MY GENERAL RANK — finds college/branch records whose historical 2024 opening-closing range contains the entered rank.
2. FIND REQUIRED RANK — shows the historical opening/closing range for a selected college and branch.

Important
---------
Historical opening/closing ranks are reference data only. They do not guarantee admission. Additional verified OJEE LE-Tech years can be added later without changing the predictor interface.
